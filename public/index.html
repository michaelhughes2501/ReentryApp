Our Mission

Rebuilding Lives, Strengthening Communities

ReentryApp was founded on a simple belief: everyone deserves a second chance. We partner with organizations, employers, and community leaders to remove the barriers that formerly incarcerated individuals face when re-entering society.

Our platform provides a single, trusted place to access vetted resources — from day-one transitional housing to long-term career development — guided by people who understand the reentry experience firsthand.
